Questions, answered

Do I need any hardware or a breadboard?

No. Every circuit is simulated and playable right in the app — though builds are real enough to copy onto your desk.

Is it good for total beginners?

Yes. Ohmie starts at "What is electricity?" and builds up one short lesson at a time.

How far does it actually go?

Eight subjects — Electronics, Analog Electronics, Digital Circuits, Semiconductors, Embedded Systems, Programming, PCB Design and Robotics — take you from Ohm's law to what's happening inside a transistor, then on to IMUs, sensor fusion and SLAM. It's the same short, hands-on lesson rhythm the whole way; the ideas just get bigger.

What if I get stuck on a question?

Ask Ohmie. Every question has a built-in explanation, plus a nudge if you need one, and signed-in subscribers can ask Ohmie follow-up questions right there on the question.

Which languages does Ohmie speak?

English, Spanish, Czech and Brazilian Portuguese. Lesson narration stays English for now.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad. There's no Android or web version.

Is there a free version?

No — Ohmie is a paid app. Pick Yearly and the first 7 days are free; cancel before day 7 and you pay nothing. Monthly is $7.99/mo. There's no ad-supported tier.

Does it work offline?

Once you're in, lessons, circuits, hints and narration all work offline. Signing up, the leaderboard, progress sync and Ask Ohmie need a connection.

Is it OK for kids?

Ohmie is rated 4+ on the App Store and written for adult beginners. Teens do fine with it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Yearly starts with a 7-day free trial — cancel before it ends and nothing is charged. Either plan can be cancelled any time in the App Store, and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
