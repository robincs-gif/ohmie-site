Questions, answered

Do I need any hardware or a breadboard?

No. Every circuit is simulated and playable right in the app — though builds are real enough to copy onto your desk.

Is it good for total beginners?

Yes. Ohmie starts at "What is electricity?" and builds up one short lesson at a time.

How far does it actually go?

Six subjects — Electronics, Robotics, Embedded Systems, Computer Architecture, PCB Design and Programming — take you from Ohm's law to what's happening inside a transistor, through a computer you build, then on to IMUs, sensor fusion and SLAM. It's the same short, hands-on lesson rhythm the whole way; the ideas just get bigger.

What if I get stuck on a question?

Ask Ohmie. Every question has a built-in explanation, plus a nudge if you need one, and signed-in subscribers can ask Ohmie follow-up questions right there on the question.

Is there a free version?

No — Ohmie is a paid app. Pick Yearly and the first 7 days are free; cancel before day 7 and you pay nothing. You'll see the price in the app before you start. There's no ad-supported tier.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Yearly starts with a 7-day free trial — cancel before it ends and nothing is charged. Either plan can be cancelled any time in the App Store, and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
