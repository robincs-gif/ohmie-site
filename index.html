Questions, answered

Do I need any hardware or a breadboard?

No. Every circuit is simulated and playable right in the app, and the builds are real enough to copy onto your desk.

Is it good for total beginners?

Yes. Start here with Electronics: lesson one is Switch it on, and it builds up one short lesson at a time.

How far does it actually go?

Fifteen subjects. Start here with Electronics and Embedded Systems. Build on it with Semiconductor, Physics, Computer Architecture, PCB Design, Robotics and Digital Logic. Go deep with Calculus, AI and Machine Learning, Circuit Analysis, Analog Electronics and Power Electronics. Programming teaches Python, and C++ is a subject of its own. It's the same short, hands-on lesson rhythm the whole way, and the ideas just get bigger.

What if I get stuck on a question?

Ask Ohmie. Every question has a built-in explanation, plus a nudge if you need one, and signed-in subscribers can ask Ohmie follow-up questions right there on the question.

Is there a free version?

Ohmie is free to download, and the lessons need an Ohmie Pro subscription, billed monthly or yearly. The yearly plan starts with a free trial for eligible new subscribers, and you'll see the price in the app before you start. There are no ads.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel any time in your Apple Account settings, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and it won't renew. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
